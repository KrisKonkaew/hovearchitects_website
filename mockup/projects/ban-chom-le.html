06 · House, Residence

Ban Chom Le

Hua-Hin, Prajuabkirikhan
Scroll

Ban Chom Le revolves around creating a wrap-around verandah, or chan, that acts as a thermal buffer for the main living spaces. This intermediate space facilitates natural ventilation, allowing wind to flow smoothly into the house.

Location
Hua-Hin, Prajuabkirikhan
Category
House, Residence

The main structure features a high, gabled ceiling designed to let warm air rise and exit through vents at the top. Cooler air is drawn in from below through perforated brick walls and a curved airflow-guiding wall, meticulously shaped based on computational fluid dynamics (CFD) simulations to maximize natural ventilation from the southwest. The double-skin façade, composed of perforated brickwork, helps exhaust rising warm air at the second-floor level.

The living area, which is most frequently used during the day, is protected by wide verandahs and extended eaves on all sides to shield the space from direct heat gain. On the west side, an additional roof louver extends beyond the eaves, filtering sunlight and creating deep shade. The ground below features grass pavers to further minimize reflected heat back into the house.

The bedroom areas are strategically located in the northeast wing, where nighttime conditions are naturally cooler. The bedroom walls are shielded from harsh afternoon sunlight, minimizing heat accumulation. Ventilated façades in front and back of the house enhance airflow, while the sloped gabled roofs in the bedroom areas direct heat away from the living spaces, ensuring thermal comfort with minimal mechanical cooling.

In addition to promoting natural ventilation, the sloped gabled roof is oriented to the west and south, offering ideal surfaces for solar panel installation.

This project embodies the essence of a true energy-efficient home — one that minimizes energy consumption in all aspects, from error-reduced construction and passive climate control to the careful selection of durable materials and low-maintenance design strategies.

Through this holistic approach, the house delivers comfort for the body, peace of mind, and long-term financial savings.
