Bangkok Boulevard Bangna KM.12

Bangna, Samutprakarn
Scroll

The architecture is defined by a single dominant form: the elliptical shape, layered and rotated at varying angles to create an engaging, organic form. The massing evokes a sculptural quality, as if the structure is blooming from the earth.

Location
Bangna, Samutprakarn
Category
Public Project

Signature patterns found on feature walls and signage are derived from the intricate textures of Desert Rose petals, reinforcing the natural, flowing aesthetic of the overall composition.

At the heart of the layout, a courtyard swimming pool provides privacy for users while enhancing natural ventilation throughout the building. The surrounding architecture offers shading, ensuring that the pool area remains usable and comfortable throughout the day.

The circulation design distinctly separates public and private users:

The main entrance is reserved for external visitors.

The secondary entrance provides direct, secure access for residents.

A Customer Service (C.S.) room is also strategically positioned to assist residents with project-related needs, enhancing both convenience and privacy.
