Bangkok Boulevard Signature Ramintra-Prayasuren

Ramintra, Bangkok
Scroll

The clubhouse at Bangkok Boulevard Signature Ramintra-Prayasuren features a distinctive white architecture, defined by flowing walls that elegantly divide the spaces.

Location
Ramintra, Bangkok
Category
Public Project

Inspired by the Barroco Museum, known for its pure white forms and organic curves symbolizing water emerging from the earth, the design captures this essence by surrounding spaces with gentle, undulating walls.

The centerpiece is a 20-meter swimming pool, partially embraced by the building’s curving forms. The sweeping white walls soften the building’s mass, create shading to reduce heat gain, and seamlessly blend indoor and outdoor spaces. Large elliptical openings carved into the walls create a dramatic sense of arrival, promote natural ventilation, and visually connect the garden with the interior spaces.

The 45-degree placement of the pool further enhances spatial dynamics — one end tucked into the clubhouse for added privacy, while the other extends outward, offering a stunning approach and a swimming experience immersed in lush greenery.

The layout draws inspiration from the concept of a museum, where every space is designed to spark inspiration. Special focal points — such as sculptural trees embraced by oval staircases — serve as visual anchors, offering moments of calm discovery throughout the complex.
