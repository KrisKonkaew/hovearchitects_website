Bangkok Boulevard Signature Sathorn Pinklao

Bangkruay, Nonthaburi
Scroll

The clubhouse design for Bangkok Boulevard Signature Sathorn Pinklao draws inspiration from the architectural elements of the renowned Musée d'Orsay in France.

Location
Bangkruay, Nonthaburi
Category
Public Project
Co-design
vector-arch

The building features a reinterpretation of the classic Mansard roof, adapted into an asymmetrical form to create a more modern and dynamic aesthetic. A sequence of colonnaded pillars enhances the verticality and grandeur of the structure, while the rhythmic repetition of geometric elements evokes the feeling of curated frames within a museum setting.

This architectural language extends seamlessly into the grand entrance gateway, unifying the entire development under a concept of timeless elegance and artistic influence.
