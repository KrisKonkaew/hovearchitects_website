Banpu Cooling District Plant

Samyan, Bangkok
Scroll

BANPU COOLING DISTRICT PLANT reimagines architecture as a living interface between people and the environment. The concept centers around creating a Public Realization Area — a space that not only supports daily functions, but also embodies the company’s Green Concept through direct experience. By maximizing usable space at ground level, integrating light-filled voids, and layering green zones with dust-trapping, oxygen-producing, and air-purifying plants, the project transforms sustainability into something tangible and engaging. Visitors don’t just see “green,” they move through it, breathe it, and understand its value.

A key architectural expression is found in the façade, composed of aluminium composite panels with color and shape gradation inspired by the Banpu logo. This element provides sun shading, airflow, and visual rhythm throughout the day — acting as a functional filter and a brand statement at once. More than just a wrapper, the façade becomes a living skin, reflecting the project’s core vision: sustainability not as an add-on, but as a natural extension of space, technology, and identity.
