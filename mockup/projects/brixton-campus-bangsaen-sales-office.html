Brixton Campus Bangsaen Sales Office

South Bangsaen Sai 4, Chonburi
Scroll

The sales office for Brixton Campus Bangsaen is located near Burapha University in Chonburi Province.

Location
South Bangsaen Sai 4, Chonburi
Category
Public Project
Co-design
vector-arch

Inspired by the natural forms of coastal waves and the subtle color transitions from sandy beaches to the edge of the sea, the building’s flowing curves introduce a sense of playfulness and energy.

The curvilinear façade design not only evokes the spirit of the seaside but also creates a dynamic and youthful atmosphere, perfectly aligned with the project’s target audience — university students seeking a vibrant living environment.
