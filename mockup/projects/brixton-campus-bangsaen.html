Brixton Campus Bangsaen

South Bangsaen Sai 4, Chonburi
Scroll

Brixton Campus Bangsaen is an eight-story condominium project comprising 246 residential units, developed by Origin Property Public Company Limited as an extension of the Brixton Campus Bangsaen Sales Office.

Location
South Bangsaen Sai 4, Chonburi
Category
Public Project
Client
Origin Property Public Company Limited
Co-design
vector-arch

Drawing inspiration from the natural rhythm of coastal waves and the delicate color transitions from sandy beaches to the sea, the building’s flowing curves bring a sense of excitement and vibrancy to the architectural form.

The development features a range of amenities, including a lobby on the ground floor, a fitness center on the seventh floor, and a rooftop swimming pool on the eighth floor, offering residents a dynamic and relaxing lifestyle connected to the spirit of the coastal environment.
