BWG Home Office

Bangwaek, Bangkok
Scroll

The BWG Home Office project integrates office and warehouse functions into a single structure while reinterpreting the typical industrial warehouse image.

Location
Bangwaek, Bangkok
Category
Public Project

Located along Bangwaek Road, the building's considerable mass posed a challenge to the design team — how to maintain a light, modern character without overwhelming the surrounding context.

By introducing strong geometric elements and vertical articulation, the façade breaks down the building’s visual bulk, making the overall form appear more refined and approachable.

A combination of white surfaces and warm wooden textures further softens the architecture, evoking the familiar sense of a modern home rather than a utilitarian storage facility. The project seeks to balance functionality with an inviting, human-centered design language that blends seamlessly into its urban surroundings.
