07 · House, Residence

Focus House

Nawamin, Bangkok
Scroll

FOCUS HOUSE is a two-story residence and tattoo studio designed around the identity and lifestyle of its owners — a young introverted couple working as tattoo artists. Rather than creating a conventional house, the project aims to establish a private sanctuary disconnected from the chaos of the outside world. The architecture is conceived as a “closed house,” using solid walls, layered spatial organization, and internal courtyards to create privacy while carefully controlling views, light, and atmosphere throughout the home.

Location
Nawamin, Bangkok
Category
House, Residence

The core concept of the project is inspired by the relationship between Positive Area and Negative Area found in tattoo art, where black ink and empty space work together to create depth, balance, and emotion on human skin. This idea is translated architecturally through the manipulation of light and shadow, voids and solid masses, as well as carefully framed openings that allow natural light to shift throughout the day. Much like a tattoo gradually revealing its character through contrast, the house uses shadow as a design medium to shape spatial experience and emotional depth.

The interior spaces are designed to seamlessly merge living and creative functions, with the Tattoo Studio and Workshop positioned as the heart of the house. Internal courtyards and layered openings bring natural light, ventilation, and greenery deep into the project while maintaining a calm and introspective atmosphere. The material palette — consisting of textured concrete, glass blocks, warm wood surfaces, and soft natural tones — reflects the raw yet delicate character of tattoo craftsmanship. More than a residence, FOCUS HOUSE becomes an architectural expression of the owners’ personality, creative process, and way of life.
