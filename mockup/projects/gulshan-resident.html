05 · House, Residence

Gulshan Resident

Gulshan, Bangladesh
Scroll

The Gulshan Resident is a private urban residence designed to respond to the dense, humid context of Dhaka while offering a refined sense of calm, privacy, and openness. The architectural massing features stacked and cantilevered volumes — each expressing distinct functions — with upper floors wrapped in timber screens for shading and privacy. The façade language reflects a balance between solidity and softness, allowing filtered light and controlled transparency while maintaining a warm, contemporary aesthetic.

Internally, the design emphasizes vertical spatial flow through sunken courtyards, elevated terraces, and a suspended swimming pool that visually anchors the house. Each level interlocks spatially, connecting formal and informal zones while maintaining clear separation between private and communal spaces. Natural ventilation, cross breezes, and landscape integration are carefully orchestrated, creating a home that is environmentally responsive and experientially rich — a multi-generational retreat in the heart of the city.
