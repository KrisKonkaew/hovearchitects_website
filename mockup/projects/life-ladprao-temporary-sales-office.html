Life Ladprao Temporary Sales Office

Ladprao, Bangkok
Scroll

The Life Ladprao Temporary Sales Office was created to serve as a provisional sales space while the main construction site was being prepared for a new building.

Location
Ladprao, Bangkok
Category
Public Project
Co-design
vector-arch

One of the project's key challenges was its location — it had to be constructed directly above an active construction traffic route. Therefore, the structure was elevated 5 meters above ground level, allowing construction vehicles to pass through at all times while simultaneously increasing the visibility of the sales office from a distance.

To address budget constraints, the design team repurposed and adapted the existing façade elements from the previous sales office, giving the new structure a fresh yet cost-effective identity that remained aligned with the project’s branding.
