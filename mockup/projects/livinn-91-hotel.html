LIVINN 91 Hotel

Pahurad, Bangkok
Scroll

LIVINN 91 Hotel is a small boutique hostel featuring six guest rooms, discreetly nestled in the heart of the vibrant Phahurat district. The project involved the transformation of an old commercial building, with a complete overhaul of its façade and interior spaces.

Location
Pahurad, Bangkok
Category
Public Project

Key architectural interventions included relocating the staircase and opening up a central courtyard to bring natural light deep into the building and enhance cross-ventilation between floors.

The interior design embraces simplicity, with a soft palette of white tones and natural wood accents, creating a warm, calming atmosphere for guests.
