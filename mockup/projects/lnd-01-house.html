03 · House, Residence

LND 01 House

Laonadi, Khonkaen
Scroll

LND01 and LND02 are two small private residences, each with a usable area of approximately 170–180 square meters, located in Laonadi Subdistrict, Khon Kaen Province. Both houses share the same plot of land and are connected through a central green space.

Location
Laonadi, Khonkaen
Category
House, Residence

The orientation of the buildings is carefully arranged to face toward the central courtyard as much as possible, while still maintaining a high degree of privacy. A front-facing layer of perforated brick walls and large wooden panels serves as a buffer, shielding the interiors from the glare of vehicle headlights coming from within the property.

Behind the wooden façade lies an expansive, double-height living area, designed to provide a sense of spaciousness and comfort, while promoting natural ventilation throughout the house.
