LND 02 House

Laonadi, Khonkaen
Scroll

LND02 is a compact two-story residence situated on the same plot as LND01, in Laonadi Subdistrict, Khon Kaen Province, Thailand.

Location
Laonadi, Khonkaen
Category
House, Residence

Designed with a focus on spatial quality and privacy, the house features a soaring double-height living area, framed by a large wooden façade and delicate louvered screens that filter views and light, offering both openness and seclusion.

The second-floor bedroom gracefully extends into the shared central garden, establishing a direct connection with the surrounding greenery. This subtle architectural gesture enhances the living experience, inviting residents to engage with nature and fostering a serene, contemplative atmosphere throughout the home.
