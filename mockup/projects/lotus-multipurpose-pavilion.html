Lotus Multipurpose Pavilion

Wat Na-Luang, Udonthani
Scroll

The Multipurpose Pavilion project at Wat Na Luang, Udon Thani Province, is inspired by the Buddhist teaching "A Blooming Lotus at the Center of the Mind," with the lotus symbolizing wisdom, growth, and liberation. The architectural design expresses this concept through the forms of blooming lotus flowers, floating lotus leaves, and rising stems reaching toward the light. The project thoughtfully integrates functional spaces, natural ventilation, and refined decorative details to create an environment that fosters Vipassana meditation, mindfulness, and genuine spiritual development.
