Matter Ngamwongwan

Rewadi, Nonthaburi
Scroll

Matter Ngamwongwan, developed by SC Asset Corporation, is a new-generation townhouse project that reflects the spirit of modern individuals — bold in self-expression yet respectful of context. The design highlights playful arch forms to convey softness and individuality, while maintaining a refined atmosphere through muted, elegant color tones. The overall composition achieves a balance between confident distinctiveness and understated sophistication.
