PVR Spa

Alvora, Portugal
Scroll

Nestled along the Vez River in northern Portugal, where the landscape transforms gently with the seasons, the project is conceived in close dialogue with its natural surroundings. Architectural masses are layered, rotated, and spaced with intention to frame the most compelling views toward the river and the distant mountains. Rather than merely organizing functions, the composition choreographs a rhythm of light, shadow, and openness that shifts gracefully throughout the day.

The design embraces the principles of light therapy as a core strategy. Natural light serves not only as an atmospheric feature but as a restorative force, scientifically proven to regulate hormonal balance, reduce stress, and enhance overall well-being. In this way, light becomes an integral part of the architecture: quiet yet powerful, fostering healing, equilibrium, and a deeper connection between people and place.
