Ruamjaidhamma Monastery

Mo Hin Khaw, Chaiyaphum
Scroll

TThis concept draws inspiration from Mo Hin Khao, often referred to as Thailand’s Stonehenge, a natural rock formation shaped over millions of years through sedimentation and erosion. The design reflects the values of time, stillness, and the quiet power of nature. Architectural forms are intentionally reduced to simple, solid masses, expressing clarity and permanence, much like stone pillars standing within a natural landscape. Emphasis is placed on mass, proportion, and carefully composed openings to create meaningful light and shadow.

Location
Mo Hin Khaw, Chaiyaphum
Category
Public Project

The design further integrates principles derived from Suan Mokkh philosophy and traditional Thai architectural “recessed geometry,” fostering an atmosphere of calm, simplicity, and contemplative use. Architectural elements are stripped of excess ornamentation, focusing instead on balance between space, light, and the surrounding environment. The result is a contemporary architectural expression that respects cultural roots and natural context, delivering a refined, restrained, and enduring identity.
