01 · House, Residence

VC House

Krungthepkreetha, Bangkok
Scroll

The project is a private 5 story residence with a usable area of 980 square meters, built on a limited land plot. The owner's objective was to maximize the building footprint within the legal constraints, while still integrating green spaces into every functional area. A vertical garden is introduced at the heart of the house, seamlessly connecting all floors and allowing natural ventilation to flow throughout the entire residence.
