Viranya Kingkaew Clubhouse

Kingkaew, Bangkok
Scroll

The clubhouse for the Viranya Bangna–Suvarnabhumi project is a 250-square-meter facility designed in a modern farmhouse style.

Location
Kingkaew, Bangkok
Category
Public Project
Co-design
vector-arch
Area
250 m²

The program includes a welcoming lobby, a fitness room, a salt-based sauna, and a half-Olympic swimming pool. The architectural inspiration draws from the traditional Western farmhouse typology, simplified through the use of geometric forms to create a design that is both modern and elegant.

With a restrained material palette and clean lines, the clubhouse embodies sophistication without unnecessary ornamentation, a peaceful yet sophisticated environment
